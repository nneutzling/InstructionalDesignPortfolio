About

I’m curious by nature.

I like exploring new ideas, asking questions, and working through sticky problems that need creative solutions.

That mindset shapes how I approach instructional design. I bring a research-informed lens to design and development, taking time to understand the learner, the context, and the real problem before deciding what the learning experience should become.

I came into instructional design from the classroom, where I learned that information is not the same as learning. My university experience in Australia, in an authentic and experiential program, deepened that belief. I design with the idea that learning becomes engaging when it feels real , when learners can connect it to their actual lives and apply it in meaningful ways.

I like the space between “I don’t know yet” and “let’s figure out what’s possible.” It is how I approach new tools, complex content, SME conversations, accessibility challenges, and AI-assisted workflows: with curiosity, persistence, and a willingness to figure things out.

I build learning that is practical, accessible, and human.
